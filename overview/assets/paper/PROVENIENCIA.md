# Materiais de papel

Criados para esta transição com a ferramenta ImageGen integrada (modo built-in), em 17/10/2026. Nenhuma imagem de terceiros foi baixada. Os dois materiais finais estão nesta pasta, sem depender do diretório externo de geração. O renderizador usa a transparência original da bola; o conteúdo da aula é deformado separadamente pela malha e pela iluminação do compositor local.

## crumpled-notebook.png

Imagem fotográfica de uma bola de papel pautado; fundo transparente.

Prompt final:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent cutout material for a realistic crumpled notebook paper transition in a local video editor. Create ONE photorealistic tightly crumpled ball made from one sheet of ordinary thin white ruled school notebook paper, photographed from directly overhead, isolated on genuine transparency. Object centered, whole object visible with generous transparent margins, approximately round but organically irregular silhouette, many intricate naturally crushed sharp folds, rolled flaps and convincing dark crevice occlusion, soft white paper fibers, realistic thin paper edges, faint light blue ruled lines and a few fragments of a red notebook margin line warped across folds. Natural matte white paper, subtle warm off-white tones, lighting from upper left with gentle fill, crisp photographic material detail. No cast shadow outside the cutout, no hands, no desk, no text, no letters, no words, no logos, no watermark. The ball must look like actual crushed notebook paper, NOT origami, not polygonal computer graphics, not a smooth sphere. Single object, no collage.

## flattened-notebook.png

Textura fotográfica das marcas de papel amassado e reaberto.

Genuine irregular network of sharp tiny crushed creases and some longer soft folds, authentic paper fibers and tiny soft shadows at crease valleys, physically realistic matte white and ivory surface. Faint pale blue horizontal notebook rules and one faint red vertical margin line near the left edge, all slightly bent by real paper creases. Photographic macro material detail under large soft diffused studio light from upper left, balanced exposure, no large dark regions, neutral white. It is FLAT on the image plane, not a ball, not origami, not a polygon mesh, no perspective, no depth of field blur. No visible sheet border, no desk, no background, no holes, no hands, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no logos. The entire frame consists only of this realistic flattened crumpled notebook paper surface.
