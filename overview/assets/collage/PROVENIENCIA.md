RIGHT: ONLY its three-bladed rotor, perfect front-facing view, three realistic long blades 120 degrees apart, each attached to one central round hub at the EXACT CENTER of the rotor's bounding box. The left mast remains separate, right rotor has no mast or background. Entire components fully in frame, no touching, no other objects, no lettering, no arrows. This is a sprite layer sheet for an animated collage, not an illustration on a page.

Gerados nesta conversa com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, sem API paga chamada pelo aplicativo.
